# Job criteria (example: copy to criteria.md and edit; criteria.md is git-ignored)

## What I want
- Work mode preference (for example: remote first, hybrid acceptable only in a target city).
- Target cities: City A, City B. Also acceptable: City C.
- Role families that fit: list the job families you would take.
- Level: the seniority band and years of experience that fit. Say what is too senior or too junior.

## Compensation
- Target base: $X-$Y, plus bonus, equity or sign-on where offered.
- A stated base under $Z is a strong negative. If pay is not stated, say so and do not guess.

## Target companies (bonus, not required)
Named: Company One, Company Two, Company Three.
Categories: industries or company types you prefer.

## Deal-breakers (set hard_dealbreaker = true)
- On-site outside the target cities with no remote option.
- A required certification or clearance you will not get.
- Posting signals chronic long hours as the norm.
- Commission-only or unpaid roles.
- Requires far more experience than you have.

## Writing voice (cover letters and rewritten bullets)
- Short, confident, specific. Lead with proof, not enthusiasm.
- No filler, no clichés, no corporate padding.
- Never frame anything as a negative. Every claim must be backed by something in the resume.
